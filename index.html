Our Purpose

Our Purpose

Harvest Mission & Charitable Trust India is dedicated to fulfilling the Great Commission. Based in the beautiful state of Kerala, India, we believe in reaching every individual with the life-changing message of Jesus Christ through direct outreach and community empowerment.

From the bustling streets to the most remote villages, we stand as a voice of hope, bringing light to those in darkness and offering a path to transformation through faith.
